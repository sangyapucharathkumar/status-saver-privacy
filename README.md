# Privacy Policy for Status Saver - Photos & Videos

Last updated: October 22, 2026

CK Digital Labs ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Status Saver - Photos & Videos mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
We do not directly collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) on our servers. All file operations, such as viewing status files and saving media to your device gallery, are performed locally on your device.

## 2. Device Storage and Files
Our application accesses local device storage solely to allow users to view status files and copy or save media directly to their device's default gallery. We do not upload, share, or store your media or files on external servers.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Our app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or deliver essential features:

* **Google AdMob:** Used to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect and use device identifier data, IP addresses, and diagnostic information to show non-personalized or personalized ads. 
  * Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
* **Google Play Billing:** Used to process in-app subscriptions. Financial transactions and payment details are handled securely and directly by Google Play. We do not store or process your financial information.
  * Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Children's Privacy
Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 5. Security
We value your trust in providing us your permissions, but remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page with an updated date.
